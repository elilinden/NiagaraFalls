A note on the rails

The Maple Leaf is one of the prettier rides in the country. Wide windows, café car, real bathrooms, and outlets at every seat. Each traveler can bring two carry-on bags free, so pack what you'll actually need. Sit on the right side heading north for Hudson River views all morning; sit on the left coming home for sunset over the Finger Lakes. The 4:55 PM Friday arrival lands you at NFS station in Niagara Falls, NY; from there a ten-minute Uber takes you across the Rainbow Bridge into Canada and your hotel. Passports out and ready at the border, easy car-window check, you'll be at Horseshoe Falls in time for dinner.

day one
Friday · May 22, 2026

Arrival & First Light

A long ride that ends with the loudest welcome of your life. By dinner you'll be standing at the brink.

6:15 AM
Pre-dawn
depart UWS $15 cab

Cab to Moynihan Train Hall

Wake up early. Coffee in a thermos. Take a yellow cab or Uber from the UWS down to Moynihan Train Hall at Penn Station, 8th Ave at 31st St. About twenty minutes at this hour. The 1/2/3 or A/C/E works too if you'd rather save the fare; the entrance is on 31st.

Pack the night before. Two carry-ons each are free on Amtrak, so pack what you'll actually need. Throw in two ponchos (you'll get one free on the boat but have backups).
7:15 AM
Departure
★ on the rails $360.40 / 2 round-trip

Board the Maple Leaf, train 63 ♡

Nine and a half hours up the Hudson, across the Mohawk Valley, past Syracuse, past Rochester, past Buffalo, into the gorge. Outlets at every seat, café car halfway down the train, real bathrooms. Sit on the right side for Hudson River views all morning. Bring a book she's been meaning to read aloud.

Snack run: Pack sandwiches from Zabar's or Citarella, a thermos, and something sweet, but the café car has decent coffee, beer, and microwaved everything if you forget. Lunch on board around noon, somewhere west of Albany.
4:55 PM
Arrival
step off free

Step off the train at NFS

The Maple Leaf pulls into Niagara Falls station on Whirlpool St (US side). Stretch on the platform. Grab the bags. Walk out front to the Uber pickup loop. You're not staying in New York tonight, you're crossing.

5:15 PM
Border
★ into Canada ~$25 Uber

Uber across the Rainbow Bridge ♡

Ten minutes in the car including border. Have passports in hand before you leave the station. The Canadian agent will ask three questions: where are you going, how long, and why. Smile, "Niagara Falls, three nights, vacation." Done. As you cross the bridge over the gorge you'll see Horseshoe Falls dead ahead, the postcard angle. This is the one that makes people cry.

Driver tip: Most Niagara Uber drivers have CDN plates and bridge accounts; ask before you book that they're cleared to cross. Falls Avenue or Fallsview Boulevard is your destination, depending on the hotel.
5:45 PM
Settle in
check in

Fallsview hotel check-in

Whichever Canadian-side hotel you booked, drop the bags, ask for the highest fall-facing room they have available, and step to the window. Yes, that's it. Change into something you don't mind getting damp. Comfortable shoes, layers, a light jacket for the gorge breeze. Take a quick photo at the door before you walk out, you'll want it later.

6:30 PM
First view
★ the postcard free

Table Rock at the brink ♡

Walk down through Queen Victoria Park to Table Rock, the lip where the Niagara River bends and pours over the Horseshoe. You are standing fifteen feet from 750,000 gallons a second. The American Falls and Bridal Veil are across the gorge, lit pale gold in the last sun. Save the boat ride for tomorrow morning, the anticipation matters.

Worth knowing: Queen Victoria Park is free, open 24/7, and stretches a mile along the rim. The Table Rock Welcome Centre has restrooms, a coffee bar, and the entrance for tomorrow's Journey Behind the Falls.
7:30 PM
Dinner
first dinner $$

Queen Victoria Place Restaurant

A historic stone restaurant inside Queen Victoria Park, set right on the rim. The patio looks straight at the Horseshoe; the dining room is dressed up enough for the first night. Order something Canadian, share a bottle of Niagara Riesling. The walk back to the hotel is two minutes.

Want something different? The Keg Steakhouse at the Embassy Suites is the casual splurge alternative; Elements on the Falls, inside the Table Rock Centre, has the closest dining-room view of the Horseshoe in the country.
9:00 PM
Night falls
★ illumination free

The Falls in color

Every night at 8 PM, twenty-one xenon spotlights come on from the Illumination Tower on the Canadian side and the water turns rose, then violet, then turquoise, then gold. Walk the rim back from Table Rock toward Clifton Hill. There are benches. The mist makes everything halo. The Canadian rim is where the lights are aimed, so you're getting the best view in the world.

10:00 PM
Fireworks
★ end of night one free

Fireworks over the gorge ♡

Five minutes. They launch from inside the gorge between the American and Horseshoe Falls and reflect off the water, the mist, and the lit-up cataract behind them. It's the longest-running fireworks series in Canada. Walk to Queen Victoria Park ten minutes early and find a patch of grass on the rim, the show goes off directly above your line of sight. Best free seat in the country, and you're on the Canadian side already.

Heads up: Fireworks run nightly from May 15 through October 12, weather permitting. Strong winds cancel; check 1-877-642-7275.
11:00 PM
Goodnight

Walk back, slowly

Hold hands. Don't talk if you don't need to. The water is still crashing in the dark behind you.

day two
Saturday · May 23, 2026

Into the Thunder

The big day. You'll get wet twice, walk through a cave of wind, and see the Falls from three countries' worth of angles.

7:30 AM
Wake
slow start

Breakfast with the view

If you booked Embassy Suites or Marriott, breakfast is included; eat fast and carb-heavy by the window. Otherwise, the Hard Rock and Hudson's at the Sheraton both face the Falls and open at 7. Carbs, coffee, repeat. Tuck the passports into a zip pocket; you'll cross before nine.

8:30 AM
Cross over
★ rainbow bridge on foot 50¢ each

Walk back to the US side ♡

From your hotel, ten minutes along the rim brings you to the foot of the Rainbow Bridge. The pedestrian crossing is twenty minutes including the booth on the US side. The walk itself is the photo-op of the trip: both Falls behind you, the gorge a thousand feet below, and a real rainbow off the mist 90% of mornings in May. American customs is faster than Canadian. Have passports out.

Foot toll is 50¢ each, US coins only. Bring four quarters before you leave NYC, or hit an ATM Friday night, the booth doesn't take cards.
9:30 AM
Cave of the Winds
★ get wet $23 each

Cave of the Winds, Hurricane Deck ♡

Take an elevator 175 feet down into the gorge, then walk up wooden walkways toward the base of Bridal Veil Falls. The closest deck (the Hurricane Deck) puts you ten feet from a falling sheet of water moving at 68 mph. They give you a poncho and special sandals. You will get drenched and you will not care.

Book first thing: Cave of the Winds uses timed tickets. Reserve the 9:30 AM slot online before you go to skip the morning rush.
11:00 AM
Boat
★ the moment $30.25 each

Maid of the Mist ♡

The most famous boat tour in North America, running since 1846. They hand you a blue poncho. The boat noses out into the gorge. You drift past the American Falls. Then the captain swings you straight into the curve of Horseshoe Falls and the world disappears into white. Twenty minutes. You will be soaked. You will not stop smiling.

Why morning: Lines are shortest before noon, you've already changed out of dry clothes after Cave of the Winds anyway, and the rainbow off the bow tends to be brightest with the sun behind you.
12:00 PM
Walk it off
stroll free

Goat Island & Three Sisters

Cross the bridge to Goat Island, the green sliver between the American and Horseshoe Falls. The Three Sisters Islands are tiny stepping-stone islets in the upper rapids; you can walk all three. Terrapin Point at the far end has the single best view of the Canadian Horseshoe Falls from the US side.

1:00 PM
Lunch
refuel $$

Top of the Falls Restaurant

Right inside the State Park on Goat Island, with a wall of windows looking onto Horseshoe Falls. Seasonal, casual, mid-range. The view does most of the work; the food is solid American. Reserve a window table.

Lighter option: Pack lunch from the morning and picnic at the Three Sisters. There are benches and zero crowds.
2:00 PM
Crossing back
★ home side again 50¢ each

Walk back over the Rainbow Bridge ♡

Same bridge, opposite direction, totally different view: now you're walking toward Horseshoe Falls instead of away from it. Twenty minutes including Canadian customs. The booth waves most pedestrians through with a quick passport scan. The first sight from the bridge into Queen Victoria Park is the one that makes people cry, even on the second crossing. You're back at your hotel in fifteen minutes.

3:00 PM
Behind
canada side ~$33 USD each

Journey Behind the Falls

From Table Rock Centre, an elevator drops 125 feet through solid rock to a tunnel that takes you behind the Horseshoe Falls. Two viewing portals let you stand directly behind the curtain of falling water. Twenty thousand gallons a second, three feet from your face. The other side has nothing like this.

5:00 PM
Power Station
★ unexpected favorite CAD $28 each

Niagara Parks Power Station ♡

Five minutes up the rim from Table Rock, an Edwardian beaux-arts cathedral built in 1905 to electrify Toronto and quietly retired in 2006. Now opened to the public as a museum: original turbines, walk-out generator floor, archival film. Then a glass elevator drops you 180 feet into the bedrock for a half-mile tunnel walk that ends behind the Horseshoe at water level, mist on your face, sun-shafts in the spray. Unique to the Canadian side, and mostly empty in the late afternoon.

Skipping the Skylon observation deck: You're eating dinner at the top of it in two hours. Don't pay twice for the view.
7:00 PM
The big dinner
★ celebrate him $$$

Skylon Revolving Dining Room ♡

Or save money and eat at Queen Victoria Place Restaurant on the patio overlooking the Falls. This is the graduation dinner. Toast him. Tell him every reason you're proud of him. He just finished law school. He's earned every word.

Reservation note: Book the Revolving Dining Room two weeks out. Ask for a window table on the inner ring, the one that revolves. The whole room turns once an hour, so you'll see the Falls illuminated from above.
10:00 PM
Fireworks, take two
★ best seat in the house free

Queen Victoria Park, front row ♡

A two-minute walk from the Skylon back to Queen Victoria Park puts you in the closest, best, free seat for the fireworks, looking right up at them as they burst over the gorge. Spread out a jacket on the lawn. Lean against him. The Falls are illuminated behind you, the fireworks are above the water, the show lasts five minutes and the memory lasts decades.

11:00 PM
Goodnight

Walk back to the hotel

Five minutes along the lit rim path, the Falls illuminated behind you the whole way. Hold hands. Don't talk if you don't need to. Goodnight to a long, soaked, perfect day.

day three
Sunday · May 24, 2026

One Last Rainbow

A slow morning, one final adventure, and the long quiet ride home with all of it still in your hair.

6:30 AM
Sunrise
★ if you can free

Sunrise rainbows at Table Rock ♡

Set one alarm. The first sunlight of the day catches the mist at the base of the Horseshoe and throws a rainbow that sometimes runs the full width of the gorge. It is empty out there at 6:30 AM. Just you, her, the water, and a half-mile of color, two minutes from your hotel door.

Trade-off: If you want to sleep in instead, that's also fine; just push everything below back an hour. You earned it.
8:30 AM
Breakfast
slow morning $

Breakfast at the hotel

If yours is included, eat well and slowly with the view. If not, take the elevator down to the lobby café and grab pastries to go, then walk to a bench in Queen Victoria Park and eat them watching the morning mist. Pack: water, sunscreen, layers; the gorge wind picks up by mid-morning.

10:00 AM
One more
★ choose your final adventure varies

Pick one big thing

Four options, depending on the kind of last hurrah you want.

1. Helicopter tour with Niagara Helicopters (CAD ~$160 each, 12 minutes, ridiculous views). The kind of thing you do once. Departs from the Canadian side, ten minutes north of the hotel.

2. Niagara Glen Nature Reserve (free, 15 min Uber north). Four kilometers of rugged trail down through a Carolinian forest of glacial boulders to the river's edge at the Whirlpool. The locals' favorite, wildly underbooked, and the closest you can get to feeling like the Falls don't exist for an hour.

3. Whirlpool Aero Car on the Canadian rim (CAD ~$19 each), a 1916 cable car that crosses the gorge at the whirlpool. Ten minutes, terrifying, beautiful, and a short walk from the Glen if you want to stack them.

4. Niagara-on-the-Lake wine country. Twenty minutes north by Uber. Peller Estates, Trius, Inniskillin (the home of icewine). Tastings start at CAD ~$15. The prettiest small town in Ontario thrown in for free.

Romantic dark horse: The White Water Walk, just north of the Whirlpool, is a boardwalk along the rapids at the foot of the gorge; you can put your hand in Class VI water. Ten Canadian dollars and easy to pair with the Aero Car.
1:00 PM
Lunch
long lunch $$

Elements on the Falls

Inside the Table Rock Welcome Centre, with the closest dining-room view of the Horseshoe in the country. Casual lunch, Canadian comfort food, and a wall of windows you'll keep glancing back at between bites. Or, if you went up to Niagara-on-the-Lake, eat there: the Old Winery Restaurant, the Pie Plate, or anywhere with patio seating on Queen Street.

3:00 PM
Slow afternoon
rest or wander free

A real afternoon off

You've earned a slow one. Options: nap at the hotel with the curtains open; walk south from Table Rock to Dufferin Islands, a quiet network of small islands connected by stone footbridges, water lilies, herons, no crowds; or walk the rim north toward Clifton Hill (the kitschy avenue of arcades, ice cream parlors, and one wax museum that takes itself too seriously). The Floral Showhouse near the Horseshoe is small, free, and surprisingly lovely.

7:00 PM
Last dinner
★ savor it $$$

Massimo's Italian Fallsview ♡

Tucked inside the Sheraton, family-run, with a menu of handmade pastas and Italian classics that out-punches every guidebook restaurant on the rim. Quieter than the revolving room, more grown-up than Friday's dinner, and the patio looks at the same Falls. Share a bottle of Niagara red, order the osso buco, walk back hand in hand.

Other Sunday-dinner moves: The Keg Steakhouse at Embassy Suites for clubby comfort, AG Inspired Cuisine in Niagara-on-the-Lake if you went up that way, or Carpaccio for old-school Italian on Victoria Avenue.

Illumination, take two

Walk the rim one more time

The xenon spotlights run again tonight, aimed straight at you on this side. Walk slowly from Table Rock toward the Skylon. Take the photo you've been meaning to take. Pick up a small, unironic souvenir from the Table Rock gift shop before it closes at ten. Look at the water for a long time. You're going to miss it.

10:00 PM
Fireworks, last call

One more set of fireworks ♡

Fireworks run nightly through October, and Memorial Day weekend means the Sunday show is a full one. Find a quieter spot than Friday's lawn: the upper terrace at Queen Victoria Park, behind the rose garden, frames the whole gorge with the Falls illuminated below. Five minutes. The last one will hit different.

Check out by 9:15. The driver crosses the bridge into the US, ten minutes including border on a Monday morning. Field Notes

Tips, Tricks & Quiet Knowing

From travel forums, locals, and the kind of hard-won knowledge that saves you twenty minutes in line and forty bucks on a poncho you didn't need.

01

WEGO + the Incline Railway, Canada side.

The WEGO bus is the Canadian-side hop-on-hop-off, $10 CAD per adult for 24 hours, blue line covers the Falls and the upper rim, red line runs north to the Whirlpool, Aero Car, and Niagara Glen. The Incline Railway ($6 CAD/day) shuttles you between Fallsview Boulevard and Table Rock so you don't grind out the hill. Either pays for itself in two rides.

02

Bring real ponchos.

The disposable ones from the boat tour will fly off you in the gorge wind. Pack reusable ponchos from Amazon ($10 each) and bring an extra dry shirt. The Hurricane Deck at Cave of the Winds will soak the inside of your jeans.

03

Niagara Falls State Park is free.

No entrance fee, ever, 24/7. You only pay for individual attractions inside (Maid of the Mist, Cave of the Winds, Observation Tower). You can walk the rim and see all three Falls without spending a dollar.

04

Get the Niagara USA Discovery Pass.

$53 per adult, includes Maid of the Mist, Cave of the Winds, Observation Tower, Aquarium of Niagara, and Niagara Gorge trolley. Saves about $25 if you do all of those, which you will.

05

Passports = many more options.

The Canadian side has the iconic head-on view, Journey Behind the Falls, the Skylon Tower, Clifton Hill, and the best fireworks viewing. Pack passports. The Rainbow Bridge crossing is fast except on Saturday afternoon, when you may want to walk back rather than wait in car traffic.

06

Memorial Day weekend = book everything now.

Hotel prices climb 30-50% over a regular weekend, and the cheap rooms vanish first. Boats and Cave of the Winds tickets sell out for prime time slots. Lock everything in this week.

07

Morning is your friend.

Most tourists do Maid of the Mist between 10 AM and 2 PM. Lines shrink dramatically before 9:30 AM and after 4:30 PM. Day 1 in this plan uses the late slot; Day 2's Cave of the Winds uses the early slot.

08

Phones go dumb at the border.

Crossing into Canada, your phone may pick up a Canadian cell tower and roam. Toggle airplane mode on as you cross, or set up T-Mobile/Verizon's free Canada day pass before you leave NYC. You don't need data with the Falls in front of you anyway.

09

Niagara is dim after 11 PM.

Once the fireworks end, the tourist district shuts fast. The 24-hour exceptions: the State Park (always open), the Casino, and the Tim Hortons on Niagara St. Plan dinner to end before 10 PM so you're at the fireworks vantage point in time.

10

The Maple Leaf is the prettiest way up.

Amtrak's Maple Leaf hugs the Hudson, climbs the Mohawk Valley, and skirts Lake Ontario. Round-trip in coach runs $360 for two with the Value discount, and you get outlets, a café car, real bathrooms, and windows the size of a car door. The way to go.

11

Charge in advance.

Bring portable batteries. The train has outlets, but at the park you'll be photographing constantly and there's nowhere to plug in. Two 10000 mAh banks split between you covers the whole weekend.

12

The Cave of the Winds sandals are gone.

As of 2024 the famous souvenir sandals were retired (eco reasons). Bring water shoes or quick-dry shoes you don't mind soaking. Old sneakers, Tevas, anything not leather.
